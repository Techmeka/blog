> The label "Product of Canada" doesn't tell you who owns the company, where the profits go, or whether the ingredients ever crossed the border. Made Around Me does.

**Made Around Me** is a pan-Canadian, product-level catalogue that answers the shelf-level question Statistics Canada can't.<!--more--> Which brands are Canadian-owned. Which are Canadian-made. And where a new domestic producer would actually move the needle.

Written for shoppers, producers, retail buyers, provincial economic development offices, journalists, and policymakers — six audiences, one dataset, six different jobs it does.
